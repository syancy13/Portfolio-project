More Than An Athlete

As a current student-athlete, it is an everyday challenge to balance academic and athletic responsibilities. On top of those responsibilies, just being a young adult and figuring out what's next can be overwhelming. That's why I created More Than An Athlete, a website that provides resources and support for student-athletes to help them navigate their next steps.
